Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version: Brevier does not collect, transmit, or sell your data. Tab audio is captured only while you run captions, and Studio reads only the file you choose. Everything is processed on your device. Free features need no account; the optional paid pass uses ExtensionPay. There are no analytics and no tracking.

What Brevier processes on your device

Brevier is a Chrome extension that shows live captions for the audio of a tab you explicitly choose, can optionally translate those captions, and can transcribe the audio track of a file you select in its Studio page. All speech recognition and translation run locally inside Chrome:

  • Tab audio. When you press Start, the extension captures the audio of the selected tab only, and only while captions are running. Audio is held in memory, processed by the on-device speech engine, and discarded. It is never recorded to disk and never transmitted.
  • Captions and translations. Recognized text and any translation are produced on your machine - with on-device models (Parakeet, Parakeet TDT, Qwen3-ASR, and optionally Hy-MT3) or with Chrome's built-in translator — and are displayed in the caption overlay. They are never sent to a server.
  • Files in Studio. A file is read only after you choose it in File Transcription Studio. Its audio track is decoded in memory, transcribed by the on-device speech engine, and then released. The original file is never copied into extension storage or transmitted.
  • Page content. The caption overlay does not read, collect, or transmit page content. It only draws the caption bar on top of the page.

What is stored on your device

Brevier saves a small amount of data in Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) so the extension works the way you configured it:

  • Your settings: engine, language, translation options, caption appearance, and Studio speech-model choice.
  • The caption bar position.
  • Your Studio Pro or Supporter status, if you bought a pass.
  • The number of free Studio transcriptions and translation passes used.
  • Finished Studio transcripts, only when Studio Pro is active. Up to 50 recent transcripts are stored in IndexedDB on this device; older ones are removed automatically. Studio Pro can delete any transcript from its history. Free file transcriptions are not saved to history, and the original file is not stored.

This data stays in your browser profile. It is never sent to Brevier's developer, and Chrome removes it when you uninstall the extension.

Studio Pro and thank-you passes

Live captions are free. Studio Pro is an optional paid upgrade, and legacy one-off Supporter passes are optional thank-you purchases. Checkout and pass management are handled by ExtensionPay, a third-party service for browser extension payments, which uses Stripe to process payments. When you buy a pass or sign in, ExtensionPay processes your purchase and stores the details needed to verify it; their privacy policy and terms apply to that processing. Brevier stores the resulting entitlement status locally. ExtensionPay's library also stores the account email, extension API key, and account user record it receives, using Chrome extension storage and its sync feature when available. Brevier contacts ExtensionPay's service over the network only to verify a purchase or open the checkout page. Brevier never receives your card details.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • tabCapture — captures the audio of the tab you start captions on, so the on-device speech engine has something to transcribe.
  • offscreen — Chrome's background service workers cannot hold audio or run the speech engines; an offscreen document does that work locally.
  • storage — saves your settings and license state on your device.
  • activeTab — lets the popup check whether the current tab can be captured and start or stop captions for it.
  • Content script on sites you visit — renders the click-through caption bar on whatever site you choose to caption. It does not read or transmit page content.

What Brevier does not do

  • No collection or transmission of audio, files, transcripts, captions, translations, or browsing activity.
  • No user accounts or sign-ins (except the optional paid pass).
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Children's privacy

Brevier is a general-purpose tool that is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
